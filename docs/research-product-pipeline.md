## Round 2 takeaways
1. **Phane, biltong, chocolate, rusks/cookies are the genuinely buyable
   everyday Botswana pantry** — everything else (sorghum flour for home
   grinding, dairy at scale) is better served by partnerships with retailers.
2. **Textile craft has a 50-year institution (Oodi)** — a single, book-able
   weaving co-op visit + co-designed capsule line is higher-trust than
   anonymous "handwoven" claims; it is the natural premium Home anchor.
3. **Gifts lane is validated at two price points** — P33–38 indulgence bars and
   P142–217 giftable moulds/pralines. Our gift box at P520 sits comfortably
   above this; keep one P150–250 add-on bar/box accessible.
4. **Excluded deliberately:** alcohol (licence/age-gating/distribution),
   therapeutic claims (Secret Kalahari-type), named-supplier marketing,
   tarred-road-only logistics beyond courier norms.
