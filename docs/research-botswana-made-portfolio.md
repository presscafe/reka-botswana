# Research: the Made-in-Botswana portfolio (as of 2026-11-11)

**Purpose.** Ground the Reka Botswana catalogue, copy and onboarding playbook in
what is *actually* produced in Botswana — not in export-brochure fantasy — so we
can curate, price and talk about real products honestly.

**Method.** Desk research only (web, 2026-11-11). No phone calls, no site visits,
no sample purchases have been made. Anything not explicitly cited here is
**UNVERIFIED**. Retail prices, staff counts and capacities below are as reported
by the cited source and have not been audited. Botswana’s legal retail/label
regime (e.g. current Consumer Protection Act commencement) is flagged where it
matters — see `docs/decisions.md`.

## 1. The institutional frame — what "Made in Botswana" means officially

| Instrument | What it is | Implication for Reka |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand Botswana / #PushaBW** (BITC — Botswana Investment and Trade Centre) | National buy-local campaign; publishes a **#PushaBW product catalogue** of locally made goods and runs the **Pride Mark**, the official symbol for locally made products/services | Our sellers should be encouraged (not yet required) to hold or apply for the Pride Mark; it is the only nationally recognised proof of local manufacture |
| **Pride Mark** (brandbotswana.co.bw) | Application via Brand Botswana website / brandbotswana@bitc.co.bw; response **within 10 working days**; product defined per the **Industrial Development Act** ("any article, thing or substance produced by any manufacturing concern"); Pride Mark requires good quality and locally made | Quality bar + local manufacturing are the two gates — both are in our maker-onboarding checklist |
| **BITC** | Integrated investment & trade promotion agency (statute); runs #PushaBW and exposes local products on shelves and internationally | Channel partner for maker onboarding and export story |
| **LEA / CEDA / NDB** | Local Enterprise Authority (business support), Citizen Entrepreneurial Development Agency (finance), National Development Bank (loans) | The right referral for makers who pass taste but lack capital/systems |
| **BIUST × AlignSure — "Made in Botswana, Sold to the World" Expo** (8–9 Oct 2026, Palapye) | Expo on taking local production to export markets; explicitly names LEA/CEDA/NDB as support system | Evidence the state treats local production as a strategic priority; good venue signal for launch |
| **Industrial Development Act definition** | "Product" = any article/thing/substance produced by a manufacturing concern | Sets the honest line: packing imported goods in Botswana is **not** local manufacture; mere rebranding is not |
| **Diamond beneficiation** (De Beers–Botswana agreement, 2023, reported) | Pledge to establish *diamond jewellery manufacturing* factories in Botswana; **P1 billion** Diamonds for Development Fund | Future luxury-gifting lane; not part of today’s artisan catalogue. Do not claim jewellery "made in Botswana" until real factories + Pride Marked products exist |

## 2. Sector-by-sector: what is verifiably made in Botswana

### 2.1 Apparel & corporate wear — strongest, most structured sector
The Botswana apparel industry is anchored by **uniforms and PPE at factory
scale**, not by designer labels:

- **Premier Clothing (Pty) Ltd** — est. 1982, Gaborone West; ~300 local staff;
  ~40,000 garments/month capacity (as reported); ISO 9001:2015; products
  registered with Dept. of Mines; SABS/BOBS certified; mining/PPE/uniforms
  focus; largest export-facing local garment capability (premier.co.bw).
- **Task Manufacturers** (Palapye) — school uniforms, bedding, curtains, PPE.
- **Justine Manufacturers** (Francistown) — boiler suits, hi-vis, flame/acid
  wear; mining/agriculture/hospitality (#PushaBW catalogue).
- **Angels Enterprises** (Gaborone) — uniforms, protective clothing, PPE,
  graduation/school attire.
- **B & M Garments** (Francistown) — 100% cotton tees, knitwear, single
  jerseys, industrial gear, headgear.
- **Supreme Textiles (Pty) Ltd** (Gaborone) — garment design/manufacture:
  suits, shirts, dresses, t-shirts, uniforms, sportswear, gowns; in-house
  cutting, sewing, **embroidery**, laundry, QA (B2B listing, prodafrica).
- **Empire Premium Custom Suits** (Gaborone) — men’s/women’s corporate suits;
  custom fit positioning.
- **Creations of Africa** (Gaborone) — corporate uniforms, promotional wear,
  gifts and giveaways (#PushaBW catalogue).
- **Ola Botswana** (Gaborone) — sports wear and caps; national-unity brand.
- **Màs Modesto** (Selebi-Phikwe, est. 2017) — tailor-made women’s wear incl.
  bridal + corporate lines announced (botswanayouth.com).
- **Nieces and Nephews** (Gaborone) — African-print children’s clothing.

**Designer/premium tier** (visibility ≠ volume): Glotto (est. 2015; first
Botswana brand represented at New York Fashion Week 2023 — Wikipedia/FWB),
Botocy (Leteisi corporate/luxury ready-to-wear), Prestige, Maru (first slow-fashion
brand), SER CONSOLADO (Maun; bridal + corporate), Indecisive, Dihdah, Kanyo M
Atelier (Gaborone), Kelbry Fashion Designs (Gaborone), Màs Modesto, Black Trash
Clothin’, MAKAY La DIVA. (Creation Africa Botswana cohort profiles; Glotto:
Wikipedia.)

**Read-through:** Botswana’s credible apparel story is *B2B corporate/workwear
at scale* plus a *bespoke tailoring* market (most "brands" above are make-to-
measure, not fashion retail like EU/US). Short-run, made-to-order B2B is the
right Reka lane — exactly what our catalogue assumes (MOQ-led quotes, 10-day
sample-to-bulk for embroidery).

### 2.2 Food & pantry — real but fragmented; mostly small processors
From the BITC #PushaBW catalogue (2020) + press:

- **Mophane-worm products** — **True Afribites** (prepacked ready-to-cook,
  ready-to-eat, "mophane bites", relish, powder; indigenous-food
  diversification), **Gabehill** (Tonota; crunchy phane 50 g snack packs up to
  1.4 kg, flavoured). Phane is genuinely Botswana (Mopane zones, Gonimbrasia
  belina).
- **Seasonings/sauces** — **Grand Salts & Sauces** (Gaborone; organic seasoning
  salts, chilli relish), **Smetchar Foods** (Gaborone; bottled chilli,
  extended shelf-life).
- **Moringa** — **Moringa Technology Industries** (Gaborone; moringa tea,
  leaf powder, dried nut, pasta, instant porridge, oil; contact
  moringabw@yahoo.com).
- **Sorghum/grains** — **Tom Pty Ltd (Seboana)** (Mochudi; sorghum flour,
  dehulled/undehulled grain, millet flour), **Crosscorn** (Phakalane; seed
  production).
- **Preserves/condiments** — The Sauce Lady (Phakalane; pastes, sauces,
  pickles), JABAMO (Molepolole; morula jams incl. with tomato/chilli/lerotse),
  Cf. **Lerotse Splash** (Gaborone; kgodu ya lerotse).
- **Indigenous fruit dairy** — **Dream Flavors** (Molepolole; indigenous-fruit
  yoghurts, sour milk, juices).
- **Confectionery** — **Kalahari Chocolates** (Gaborone; Belgian-style
  couverture; "award winning" per catalogue bio — award details UNVERIFIED).
- **Meat** — no branded seswaa/ready-meal processor verified in desk research;
  seswaa remains home/retail-butchery trade. **Seswaa spice rubs** are the
  viable SKU lane (matches catalogue).
- **Honey** — **Kalahari Honey (Chabana Farms)** — human-elephant-conflict
  hive model; raw desert honey + Botswanan mead; reported supply into 100+
  pharmacies/supermarkets in BW/NA/ZM and 500+ women jobs (2019–2021 press).
  **Founder Mavis Nduchwa died Aug 2021 (Wikipedia). Current brand status and
  ownership are UNVERIFIED.** Do not claim "bottled by Kalahari Honey" until
  verified; our Kang apiary is a fictional stand-in and must remain so
  documented.
- **Morama** (Tylosema esculentum) — genuinely Botswana (Kgalagadi/Kalahari);
  Wikibooks lists morama bean as classic local staple. **No verified
  commercial morama-bar manufacturer found** — our "Morama Crunch" SKU is a
  concept, not a sourcing-ready product. Mark UNVERIFIED.
- **Dairy at scale** — **Sally Dairy (Tlokweng, ~120 staff)**; distribution via
  Choppies/Spar/Pick ’n Pay/Sefalana; plus Aquafill water. Not an artisan
  channel; note as evidence of local food-manufacturing maturity.
- **Juices/beverages** — Oasis Beverages (water/juices), Health Generation
  (juices/wraps, Gaborone), Native Foods (mosukujane tea, Gaborone).

### 2.3 Beauty, body & soap — small natural makers are the norm
- **Le Afrika / La Afrika** (Gaborone) — African black soap, shea soap,
  unrefined shea; 100% natural, vegan, cruelty-free positioning.
  **Kgalagadi Soap Industries ("Marang", est. 1978)** — bar/liquid soaps at
  scale.
- **Naga Earth** (Gaborone) — marula/baobab/kalahari-melon oils; launched
  **Naga Naturals** (2021) importing bulk carrier oils/butters for local
  manufacturers — i.e. local makers partly use imported inputs; honest
  labelling should disclose input origin.
- **Phytoafrica** — unrefined marula/baobab/mafura butters; bulk & small qty,
  export-oriented (phytoafrica.com).
- **DLG Naturals** — morula indigenous oil + Botanical ingredients export;
  seed cake to feed.
- **Divine Morula** (Gabane) — morula juice, concentrates, jams, ice pops;
  morula lip/body line (scrubs, butter, lotion).
- **Nubian Seed** (Gaborone) — natural butters, carrier/essential oils, clays,
  soaps.
- **Ludo Beauty** (Gaborone) — plant-based hair care (conditioner, shampoo).
- **Asali Hair & Body** — "proudly manufactured and packaged in Botswana".
- **Secret Kalahari** (Gaborone) — donkey-milk skincare; makes medical
  claims (asthma/diabetes etc.) — **do not copy claims; therapeutic claims on
  cosmetics are not permitted without medicine registration**.
- **Pro TM** (Gaborone) — lodge amenities (showgel, lotion).
- Household/cleaning: Jess Manufacturing (Linex, Gaborone), Clover Chemical,
  Exclusive Chem (CIT-owned), Kgalagadi Soap Industries.

### 2.4 Craft & basketry — premium export craft with a single origin story
- Coil-woven palm baskets from the **Okavango Delta / Panhandle (Etsha
  villages, Hambukushu and Bayei weavers)** are the known Botswana craft
  export. Team sizes, materials: palm fibre, natural dyes, bone awl; one-of-a-
  kind pieces; **large pieces can take up to a year**; export retail listings
  observed at **US$290–360** (~P4,000–5,000) (Bots Quality Baskets, Maun —
  qualitybaskets.co.bw; Brighton & Hove Museums "Making Botswana" notes —
  Botswanacraft, Gaborone, markets baskets by Hambukushu women from Etsha;
  Okavango Baskets MD Ms Gabotsholwe Selemilwe Ntwe).
- **Botswanacraft** (Gaborone) — established market/retail channel for
  baskets and crafts; RPMT collection acquired through them.
- **A Heritage Legacy** (Gaborone) — bags from **Mokolwane palm + Tswana
  leather** — a palm/leather hybrid craft lane.

**Read-through:** real market price for a display-grade Okavango basket is
**P1,500–P5,000+**, not P950. Our catalogue basket price is below market
(see §4). Craft labour minutes and one-of-a-kind status justify premium
positioning and "collected per order, photographed on request" fulfilment.

### 2.5 Leather goods — small citizen-owned workshops, strong identity
- **Dinny’s Leatherworks** (Palapye) — belts, bags, sandals, traditional
  attire, mats; also runs leather training (#PushaBW catalogue).
- **LT Leathers** (Metsimotlhabe) — bags, gloves, aprons, hats from "Tswana
  leather".
- **Moahi Products** (Gaborone) — artisan leather "highest quality, unique
  design".
- **Kings Leather Works** (Letlhakeng) — shoes; retail + corporate.
- **Rata Letlalo** (est. 2016, Gaborone) — handmade leather goods.
- **Afromuse Designs** (Gaborone) — saddle-stitched bags, indigenous tanning.
- **Instyle Trends** (Gaborone) — leather shoes/bags/accessories, customised.
- **A Heritage Legacy** (Gaborone) — Mokolwane palm + leather bags.

### 2.6 Diamond/luxury lane — watch, don’t enter yet
De Beers–Botswana agreement (2023, reported): commitment toward *diamond
jewellery manufacturing factories in Botswana*; P1bn Diamonds for Development
Fund; global sightholders relocated to Gaborone over the last decade. **No
consumer-grade "Made in Botswana" diamond jewellery line exists yet** — do not
list jewellery today; keep "Reka Signature Gifting" door open (corporate
gifting with certified-local lines) once factories + Pride Mark exist.

## 3. Market reality checks (flagged honestly)

- **Import dependence is structural.** ~65% of Botswana’s imports reportedly
  originate in South Africa (accio.com market brief, 2026-08-25 — low-quality
  source; treat as directional only, **UNVERIFIED**). Government is pushing
  localisation; Chinese-owned firms upgrading from trade to "made in
  Botswana" manufacture (2018+ reports).
- **Quality perception is a live issue.** "Made in Botswana" competes with
  cheap imports; Pride Mark exists precisely to close that trust gap. Our
  curation + verification language mirrors this.
- **Scale is small.** Most named makers are micro-enterprises; capacity,
  consistency and paperwork (invoices, VAT, BOBS where applicable, food
  safety) are the onboarding friction — not interest.
- **Verification anchors** available today: Pride Mark (Brand Botswana),
  LEA registration, CEDA/NDB relationship, BOBS certification (as held by
  Premier Clothing for SABS/BOBS), BITC export listings.

## 5. Maker-onboarding playbook (grounded in research)

**Gate 0 — Identity (all):** business registration or cooperative/trust
papers; LEA registration encouraged; Pride Mark application (10 working days);
BITC export listing if exporting.
**Gate 1 — Proof of local manufacture (Industrial Development Act test):**
workshop address + right to operate; what is actually done in-country
(cut/sew/mill/cure/dye — not repack/relabel); input-origin disclosure where
material (e.g. shea kernels, fragrance, fabric).
**Gate 2 — Quality/safety:** BOBS where applicable (food, soaps); ISO
9001/SABS/BOBS examples exist locally (Premier Clothing); food processors:
premises + labelling compliance, allergen statements, no unregistered
therapeutic claims (Secret Kalahari is the cautionary example).
**Gate 3 — Commercial reality:** price list, lead time, weekly capacity,
returns handling, willingness to invoice (cash-or-transfer only), WhatsApp
responsiveness; sample + photos before listing.
**Gate 4 — Pride Mark / IP:** if using tradition-derived designs (e.g. basket
patterns), confirm community protocol and that the cooperative (not a middle
person) sets floor price — our basket listing wording already reflects "co-op
own pricing".

**Outreach priority (from research):** Moringa Technology Industries
(Gaborone) → DLG Naturals / Phytoafrica (marula oil) → Gabehill (phane snack
packs) → Bots Quality Baskets co-ops via Botswanacraft (Gaborone) → Dinny’s
Leatherworks (belts, training) → Grand Salts & Sauces → Creations of Africa
(corporate gifting). All contacts from cited public sources; verify before any
MOU. **No outreach has been made yet.**

## 6. What we will NOT claim (until evidence exists)

- "Botswana's largest/only X" — no audited data; superlatives banned in copy.
- Named-supplier claims ("made by Kalahari Honey") — partner names appear on
  pages only after written partner approval.
- Health/therapeutic effects for food/cosmetics (moringa, honey, marula,
  baobab) — food-grade language only; pharmacy-grade claims need registration.
- "Carbon-neutral / plastic-free" — unless maker certifies; say "recyclable
  kraft" instead (we do).
- VAT/pricing law specifics beyond "prices in pula; VAT invoices on request".
- Consumer Protection Act **year/number** — sources conflict (1998 vs 2018);
  the site now cites the Act without a year. Confirm commencement against the
  statute book pre-launch.
- Kalahari Honey volume/jobs figures (2019–2021 press; founder deceased
  2021-08; current ownership UNVERIFIED).
- Export share / import ratios ("65% from South Africa" — weak source).
